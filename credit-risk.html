01

Problem

Credit risk is spread across several data sources and cannot be assessed reliably from a single attribute.

The project needed to consolidate basic customer information, transaction behaviour, and borrowing history into a consistent analytical view. The resulting model had to support both individual credit scoring and broader customer segmentation for approval, monitoring, and risk control.

02

Method

A pipeline from fragmented records to an evaluated and usable risk assessment.

  1. 01Integrate multi-source customer data
  2. 02Clean missing values and inconsistent records
  3. 03Select and engineer risk-related features
  4. 04Build scoring and risk-segmentation models
  5. 05Evaluate performance and translate findings

Customer information

Basic profile attributes provided context for understanding differences between customers.

Transaction behaviour

Transaction records contributed behavioural signals that could not be captured by profile data alone.

Lending records

Borrowing information added evidence about existing obligations and repayment-related risk.

Evaluation

The models were assessed for analytical usefulness before their outputs were connected to business recommendations.

03

Output

Risk information organized into outputs that support repeatable decisions.

  • 01
    Multi-dimensional credit profile

    A consolidated representation of customer, behavioural, and borrowing characteristics.

  • 02
    Credit scoring model

    A quantitative basis for comparing and prioritizing applicants.

  • 03
    Risk segmentation

    Customer groups defined to support differentiated review and monitoring.

  • 04
    Model evaluation report

    A structured assessment of model behavior and practical limitations.

  • 05
    Business recommendations

    Suggested applications in credit approval, customer segmentation, and risk control.

Source data and model artefacts are not published because credit and lending records can contain sensitive financial information.

04

What it demonstrates

Connecting data engineering, risk modeling, model evaluation, and business communication.

The project demonstrates how heterogeneous records can be cleaned and structured for modeling, how a credit profile can support risk segmentation, and how evaluation findings can be translated into concrete recommendations for approval and portfolio monitoring.
